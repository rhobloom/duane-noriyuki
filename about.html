Duane Noriyuki is a writer and photographer whose work has appeared in newspapers and publications across the country, including the Detroit Free Press, St. Paul Pioneer Press, and Los Angeles Times.

This site is being built as a living archive of that work: journalism, essays, photographs, and eventually new writing.

The organizing idea is simple: not everything worth preserving arrives neatly in chronological order. Stories connect through people, places, objects, memory, community, and the act of looking again.

working idea

Look again. Look closer. Notice what everyone else has stopped seeing.
