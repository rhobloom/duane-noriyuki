About me.

I’m a writer and photographer. Over the years I’ve written for the Detroit Free Press, St. Paul Pioneer Press, and Los Angeles Times.

Reporting took me into the lives of strangers, families, artists, and communities going through change. I also spent years teaching writing, including to young people in the juvenile justice system.

I live on the McKenzie River now. I still write and take pictures. These days I often have my camera out for the river, the garden, or a change in the light.

I’ve gathered my journalism, photographs, and personal writing here so I can share them with you.
