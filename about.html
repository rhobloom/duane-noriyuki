Duane Noriyuki is a writer and photographer whose work has appeared in newspapers and publications across the country, including the Detroit Free Press, St. Paul Pioneer Press, and Los Angeles Times.

His work has taken him into the lives of strangers, families, artists, young people, and communities in moments of change. He has also spent years teaching writing, including with young people in the juvenile justice system.

Duane now lives on the McKenzie River and is slowly becoming part of the land. He still writes and takes photographs, paying attention to the river, weather, gardens, light, and the lives around him.

This site brings those pieces of work together: journalism, essays, photographs, and new writing as it arrives.
