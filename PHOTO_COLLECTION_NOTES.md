# Duane Noriyuki Photography Collection — Collection Notes

This version of the website incorporates the first thumb-drive collection without altering the preserved originals.

## What arrived

- 222 files total
- 217 image files (JPG/JPEG)
- 4 MOV video files
- 1 folder-level archive structure after extraction

## First-pass organization

The original thumb-drive files are intentionally not reorganized or renamed.

For the public website gallery:

- 161 filename-level photographs are represented
- obvious duplicate filenames and alternate copies are consolidated to one representative image
- the original copies remain preserved outside the website's optimized image assets
- the four MOV files are preserved but not yet incorporated into the public photography page

## Important archival note

The thumb drive is treated as source material, not as a declaration that every image is automatically intended for public release. The website can therefore be curated further with Duane as he identifies photographs that are private, family-only, or otherwise not intended for publication.
